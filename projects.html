Selected work

2026Field experiments

SPaT / R2X-assisted crosswalk navigation

Integrated ROS 2, Nav2 and a hybrid safety node to make crossing decisions from signal phase and remaining green time. Compared SPaT and vision-based navigation under daytime, nighttime and dynamic occlusion conditions.

  • ROS 2
  • Nav2
  • SPaT / R2X
  • Time-to-Cross
2026Thesis research

VLM-based intersection context understanding

Investigating infrastructure-camera scene understanding for AMR waiting, entry and crossing decisions. The thesis plan covers pedestrians, vehicles, crosswalk occupancy and occlusion, with simulation and scenario-based evaluation.

  • VLM
  • Infrastructure cameras
  • AMR
  • Simulation
2026System integration

LiDAR navigation & cloud robot experiments

Worked on LiDAR mapping, localization and Nav2 integration for mobile robots, including FAST-LIO navigation experiments at KAIST Munji Campus.

  • FAST-LIO
  • LiDAR
  • SLAM
  • Nav2
2026Simulation development

Delivery robot digital twin

Configured an Isaac Sim delivery robot pipeline with LiDAR, IMU, camera, odometry, joint states and ROS 2 communication for navigation scenarios.

  • Isaac Sim
  • ROS 2
  • Sensor integration
2025Research prototype

LLM-guided planning in nuPlan

Prototyped an LLM context interpreter that adjusts rule-based PDM planner parameters. Implemented an uncooperative overtaking scenario in nuPlan and compared behavior with a mode-selection baseline.

  • nuPlan
  • LLM
  • PDM planner
  • Long-tail scenarios
2025Research & conference posters

Multi-camera pedestrian trajectory perception

Combined YOLOv8, DeepSORT, Re-ID and homography across five CCTV views to study consistent global IDs and pedestrian trajectory recovery.

  • YOLOv8
  • DeepSORT
  • Re-ID
  • Homography
