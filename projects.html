Selected projects that represent my work on communication-assisted AMR navigation, ROS 2 robotic systems, LiDAR-based SLAM, and infrastructure-aware perception.

Representative Work

SPaT-Assisted AMR Crosswalk Navigation

A ROS 2-based framework integrating Nav2, SPaT/R2X messages, and a hybrid safety node for signal-aware AMR crossing.

ROS 2 · Nav2 · SPaT · R2X · AMR

LiDAR-based Robot Navigation with Fast-LIO2 and Nav2

Integration of LiDAR SLAM, map generation, localization, and Nav2-based autonomous navigation for mobile robot operation.

Fast-LIO2 · LiDAR · SLAM · Nav2 · ROS 2

Isaac Sim-based Delivery Robot Digital Twin

Simulation environment for Delivery Robot sensing, ROS 2 communication, LiDAR/IMU/camera configuration, and scenario validation.

Isaac Sim · ROS 2 · LiDAR · IMU · Camera

Traffic Light Perception for Mobile Robots

Vision-based traffic signal recognition using object detection, color-state classification, and temporal smoothing for robot navigation.

YOLO · RGB Camera · Traffic Signal Recognition · ROS 2

Multi-Camera Pedestrian Trajectory Perception

A perception pipeline combining YOLO, DeepSORT, Re-ID, and Homography Transform for full pedestrian trajectory recovery.

YOLO · DeepSORT · Re-ID · Homography

V2X/C-ITS Field Experiment Analysis

Participation in V2X-based C-ITS scenario experiments for intersection blind-spot incidents, including GNSS trajectory analysis.

V2X · C-ITS · GNSS · Field Experiment
